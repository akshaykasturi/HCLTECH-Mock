# Evaluation report

Each source is reported **separately**. Generator-holdout tickets come from the same scenarios as the training data, so their scores are optimistic. Hand-written, paraphrased and novel tickets are the real test.

**Who wrote the test data:** the hand-written test tickets (`data/test/handwritten_tickets.csv`) and the validation tickets (`data/validation/validation_tickets.csv`) were written by the AI assistant that helped build this project, not by real users. They use natural wording unlike the generator's templates, but they are not a substitute for real tickets.

## Known limitations

- Generator-holdout scores are optimistic: same scenarios and phrase pools as training.
- Calibration makes confidence honest for familiar tickets only. For unseen problem types the classifier can still be confident and wrong, which is why the retrieval gate and min(signals) exist.
- The retrieval-strength threshold (0.73) was tuned on 35 validation tickets (`scripts/tune_gate.py`), never on these test tickets. 35 is small, so the threshold is a rough estimate.
- The hand-written and validation tickets were written by the AI assistant, not by real users.
- Priority accuracy on generator tickets is inflated: impact phrases come from a fixed pool per priority.
- Cosine similarity depends on the embedding model: changing EMBED_MODEL needs a newly tuned threshold.
